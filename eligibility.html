    TRANSPARENT REQUIREMENTS

    Required Documents By Profile

    Select your employment or enterprise type to view the exact documentation required.

    Documents For Salaried Individuals

    Permanent employees of government entities, public listed corporations, private limited firms, or recognized SMEs.

    Identification: Copy of National Identity Card (NIC) or valid Sri Lankan Driving License / Passport.
    Income Proof: Latest 3 months salary slips certified by employer or HR department.
    Bank Statements: Last 3 to 6 months bank statement showing regular salary credits.
    Address Verification: Recent utility bill (electricity, water, or landline phone) within last 3 months.
    Vehicle Quotation: Invoice/quotation from vehicle vendor or copy of Vehicle Registration Certificate (CR).

    Documents For Business Owners & Companies

    Sole proprietors, partnerships, and Private Limited companies expanding fleet operations or acquiring executive transport.

    Business Registration: Valid Certificate of Business Registration (BR) or Form 1 / 20 for Limited liability companies.
    Bank Statements: 6 to 12 months commercial company bank statements showing enterprise cash flow.
    Directors' / Proprietors' NIC: Copies of National Identity Cards of all active partners or directors.
    Financial Records: Audited or management accounts for the past 1 to 2 financial years (for large fleet requests).

    Documents For Self-Employed & Professionals

    Doctors, Chartered Accountants, Attorneys-at-Law, IT Freelancers, Architects, and Engineers.

    Professional Proof: Membership certificate of SLMC, Bar Association, CA Sri Lanka, or relevant institute.
    Income Track Record: 6 months bank statements demonstrating client payments or professional billings.
    Identification: NIC and residential address utility billing.

    Documents For Sri Lankan Expatriates

    Non-resident Sri Lankans working in Middle East, Europe, Australia, or Asia sending remittances home.

    Passport & Visa: Valid passport copy, employment visa, and overseas employment contract.
    Remittance Evidence: 6 months official inward remittance slips or bank statement showing regular foreign inflow.
    Local Co-Borrower: NIC of resident spouse, parent, or designated power of attorney holder in Sri Lanka.
